# Code of Conduct

BetterBauang is an open civic-tech project. Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully and keep collaboration focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss evidence, implementation, and product decisions rather than attacking people.
- Respect political and civic differences.
- Do not use the project to campaign for or against candidates, parties, or factions.
- Do not publish private, sensitive, leaked, or unlawfully obtained information.
- Raise sourcing, accuracy, security, accessibility, and maintenance concerns directly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, and attempts to use the project for partisan campaigning are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may remove contributions, comments, or participation that violate these expectations.
